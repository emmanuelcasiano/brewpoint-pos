BrewPoint database schema

Every table and column behind the POS, the shop back-office and the BrewPoint staff console, grouped by area. Click a table to trace what it points to and what points to it.

  • One database, many shopsEvery business table carries tenant_id and every query filters on it. Those links are listed but not drawn.
  • IDs made on the devicePrimary keys are UUIDs a register can create offline. Device-written rows also carry device_id, client_created_at and synced_at.
  • Money and stock as numbersMoney is integer centavos (bigint). Stock is in the item's base unit: ml, g or pc.
  • Stock is a ledgerstock_movements records every change. item_branch_settings.on_hand is its cached total.
  • Staff are not shop usersStaff sign in as platform_users. They see inside a shop only through an approved support_access_grants row, and every action goes to platform_audit_log.
  • Billing is provider-neutralStripe now, PayMongo or Xendit later: billing_customers, payment_methods and payment_events name their provider. Prices are versions in plan_prices.
  • Nothing important is deletedSales are voided, orders cancelled, users deactivated, devices revoked. audit_log is append-only.
